Why Reading Matters for Future English Teachers

Reading is a gateway to knowledge, culture, and communication. For future English teachers, strong reading skills are essential because they allow educators to access academic texts, understand diverse perspectives, and model effective comprehension strategies for their students.

Reading also develops critical thinking, enriches vocabulary, and strengthens grammar awareness, making it a cornerstone of language learning.

To teach reading effectively, teachers must cultivate personal reading habits that make them confident, resourceful, and inspiring in the classroom. Here are some tips:

  • Read regularly in English – mix academic, literary, and everyday texts.
  • Practice active reading – highlight, annotate, and question.
  • Explore different genres – novels, articles, essays, reports.
  • Summaries help reinforce comprehension.
  • Discuss readings to deepen understanding.
