Why Reading Matters for Future English Teachers

Reading is a gateway to knowledge, culture, and communication. For future English teachers, strong reading skills are essential because they allow them to access academic texts, understand diverse perspectives, and model effective comprehension strategies for their students.

Reading also develops critical thinking, enriches vocabulary, and strengthens grammar awareness, making it a cornerstone of language learning.

But to teach reading effectively, teachers must cultivate strong personal reading habits. Here are some practical tips:

  • Read regularly in English – choose academic, literary, and everyday texts.
  • Practice active reading – highlight key ideas and take notes.
  • Expand genres – novels, articles, essays, professional texts.
  • Summarize and reflect on what you read.
  • Discuss readings with peers to deepen understanding.
