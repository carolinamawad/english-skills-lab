Why Reading Matters for Future English Teachers

Reading is a gateway to knowledge, culture, and communication. For future English teachers, strong reading skills are essential because they allow educators to access academic texts, understand diverse perspectives, and model effective comprehension strategies.

Developing reading habits makes teachers confident and resourceful. Here are some tips:

  • 📘 Read regularly – mix academic, literary & everyday texts.
  • 📝 Highlight and take notes to reinforce understanding.
  • 📚 Explore different genres.
  • ✍️ Write summaries to strengthen comprehension.
  • 💬 Discuss readings to deepen interpretation.
