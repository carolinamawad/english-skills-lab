Why Reading Matters for Future English Teachers

Reading builds knowledge, critical thinking, and teaching confidence.

Reading is a gateway to knowledge, culture, and communication. For future English teachers, strong reading skills are essential because they allow educators to access academic texts, understand diverse perspectives, and model effective comprehension strategies for their students. Reading also develops critical thinking, enriches vocabulary, and strengthens grammar awareness, making it a cornerstone of language learning.


But in order to teach reading effectively, we must first cultivate our own reading habits. Developing personal reading proficiency enables teachers to feel confident, resourceful, and inspiring in the classroom. Here are some practical tips to improve your reading skills:

  • Read regularly in English – choose a mix of academic, literary, and everyday texts.
  • Practice active reading – highlight key ideas, take notes, and ask questions.
  • Expand genres – explore novels, articles, essays, and professional texts.
  • Summarize and reflect – write short summaries to reinforce comprehension.
  • Discuss readings with peers to deepen understanding.
