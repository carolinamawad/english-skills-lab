Classroom English

As future English teachers, it is essential to feel confident using English naturally in the classroom. Classroom English helps you manage lessons, give instructions, and interact with students effectively. These tips and examples will help you create an immersive environment where English is the main language of communication.

Tips and Best Practices

Start with clear routines

  • "Good morning, everyone. Let’s get started."
  • "That's all for today. See you next class."

Give instructions step by step

  • "Open your books to page 10."
  • "Work in pairs. Ask and answer the questions."

Encourage participation

  • "Excellent answer!"
  • "Who would like to try?"

Manage classroom behavior politely

  • "Please raise your hand before speaking."
  • "Let's stay focused on the activity."

Check understanding

  • "Can you repeat the instructions in your own words?"
  • "Do you understand what to do?"

Promote English immersion

  • "May I go to the bathroom?"
  • "Can you help me, please?"
