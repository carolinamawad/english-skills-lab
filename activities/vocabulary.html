Why Vocabulary Matters for Future English Teachers

Vocabulary is the heart of language learning, as it provides the words and expressions needed to communicate ideas clearly and effectively. For future English teachers, a strong command of vocabulary is essential because it allows them to explain concepts precisely, enrich classroom discussions, and help learners expand their linguistic repertoire.

A rich vocabulary also enables teachers to design engaging activities, connect language to real-life contexts, and support students in developing both academic and everyday communication skills. By teaching vocabulary strategically, educators empower learners to express themselves with confidence and to access a wider range of texts and opportunities.

But in order to teach vocabulary effectively, we must first strengthen our own knowledge. Building a broad and flexible vocabulary helps teachers feel confident, creative, and resourceful in the classroom. Here are some practical tips to improve your vocabulary:

  • Read a variety of texts – novels, articles, and academic papers expose you to diverse vocabulary.
  • Keep a personal word journal – write down new words, their meanings, and example sentences.
  • Use flashcards or apps – spaced repetition helps reinforce long-term retention of vocabulary.
  • Practice active use – incorporate new words into your writing and speaking to make them part of your active vocabulary.
  • Explore synonyms and word families – understanding related words deepens comprehension and expands expression.
